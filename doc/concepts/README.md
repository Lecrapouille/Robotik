# 💡 Concepts

[⏫ Documentation](../README.md)

A concept page explains why: the problem, the idea that solves it, the
choice that RobotIK made and what it costs. Read these pages when you want
to understand, not when you want to do something now.

- [Robotics in ten minutes](robotics-in-ten-minutes.md): the words of
  robotics (joint, frame, TCP, kinematics, skill, behavior tree) and what
  RobotIK does with them. Start here if robotics is new to you.
- [Worlds, scenarios and experiments](worlds-scenarios-experiments.md): why
  a run is described by three files, and the difference between the truth,
  the description and the beliefs.
- [Skills, the scheduler and behavior trees](skills-and-behavior-trees.md):
  who decides what to do, who decides if it can run now, and who knows how.
- [The PDDL planner](pddl-planner.md): a goal instead of a behavior tree,
  how a plan runs, and its limits.
- [Tools and frames](tools-and-frames.md): flange, `tool0`, `tool_mount`,
  TCP, and why the robot and the tool stay two files.
- [Architecture](architecture.md): the layers, one step of the simulation,
  the backends, and who owns which state. For people who change RobotIK
  itself.
