# 💡 Concepts

[⏫ Documentation](../README.md)

A concept page explains why: the problem, the idea that solves it, the choice that RobotIK made and what it costs. Read these pages when you want to understand, not when you want to do something now.

The pages are numbered in reading order. Each page uses the words of the pages before it.

1. [Robotics in ten minutes](01-robotics-in-ten-minutes.md): the words of robotics (joint, frame, TCP, kinematics, skill, behavior tree) and what RobotIK does with them. Start here if robotics is new to you.
2. [Worlds, scenarios and experiments](02-worlds-scenarios-experiments.md): why a run is described by three files, and the difference between the truth, the description and the beliefs.
3. [Tools and frames](03-tools-and-frames.md): flange, `tool0`, `tool_mount`, TCP, and why the robot and the tool stay two files.
4. [Skills, the scheduler and behavior trees](04-skills-and-behavior-trees.md): who decides what to do, who decides if it can run now, and who knows how.
5. [The PDDL planner](05-pddl-planner.md): a goal instead of a behavior tree, how a plan runs, and its limits.
6. [Architecture](06-architecture.md): the layers, one step of the simulation, the backends, and who owns which state. For people who change RobotIK itself.
